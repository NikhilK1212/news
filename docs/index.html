The film reimagines the unfinished 1936 work of the same name by the Spanish poet and playwright Federico García Lorca (1898-1936), based on the only four existing pages. It would have been Lorca’s first play with a gay protagonist. La bola negra is also co-written by Los Javis in collaboration with queer playwright Alberto Conejero, author of the original play La Piedra Oscura (The Dark Stone, 2013) on which the film is also partly based.
