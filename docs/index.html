  • Section 179 vs Bonus Depreciation
    Section 179 vs bonus depreciation comes down to one question: do you want to decide exactly how much equipment cost to deduct this year, or are you happy with the default? Section 179 is an election you make item by item, and it is capped by your business income. Bonus depreciation, which the IRS calls the special depreciation allowance, applies automatically to qualified property unless you elect out by class of property. If this year is profitable and you want to fine-tune the number, Section 179 gives you the steering wheel. If you want the full first-year write-off with less paperwork, or your purchases are bigger than this year’s business income, bonus depreciation usually does the heavy lifting.
