  • recycled aerospace steel and rammed earth shape homeware collection for life on mars
    Roc H Biel presents Martian Relics, a collection of five domestic objects that examines how everyday life might evolve if humans settled on Mars. Comprising a plate, mirror, window, flower pot, and water filter, the project uses speculative design to explore how environmental conditions influence culture, memory, resource use, and human behavior. Constructed from rammed earth and recycled stainless steel, the objects were photographed in the volcanic landscape of Lanzarote, whose terrain closely resembles the Martian surface. Rather than presenting a vision of future living on Mars, the collection reflects on contemporary attitudes toward Earth and the renewed interest in space colonization. Each object addresses a different aspect of human existence, considering how beliefs, identity, knowledge, nostalgia, and access to resources might be reshaped in an arid environment where materials and water are scarce.
