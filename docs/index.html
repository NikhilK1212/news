  • FSSAI Proposes Red Warning Labels on Packaged Foods High in Sugar, Salt and Fat — TrendKia
    Major regulatory changes are underway for packaged and processed food items sold across the country. The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India has informed the Supreme Court that it intends to mandate clear red warning labels on the front panels of food products that contain excessive amounts of sugar, salt, or saturated fat. This regulatory initiative is designed to provide consumers with easily understandable warnings about food items where the presence of these concerning nutritional components exceeds recommended thresholds. According to the compliance affidavit submitted to the apex court, the guidelines are anchored in the dietary guidelines for Indians issued for the year 2024 by the Indian Council of Medical Research and the National Institute of Nutrition. Under this framework, if any food product contains excess levels of at least two out of three specified nutritional concerns—namely high saturated fat, high sugar, or added salt—a prominent red warning box will be required on the front of the packaging.
